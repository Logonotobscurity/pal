# LOG_ON Research Evidence Notebook

Status: ACTIVE — initialized 2026-09-29

This directory is the durable research-control plane for PAL/LOG_ON work.

## Separation rules
- Raw/source material is evidence.
- Derived notes are synthesis.
- Claims are atomic propositions with provenance.
- Hypotheses are testable and must not be presented as established findings.
- Unresolved disagreement remains visible.
- Code/docs prove implementation intent or state, not empirical efficacy.

## Lifecycle
REGISTER → INGEST → EXTRACT CLAIMS → CLASSIFY → LINK EVIDENCE → COMPARE → RECORD CONTRADICTIONS → PROPOSE DELTA → HUMAN REVIEW → APPROVE → LINT → NEXT TEST

- RN-02 PAL Cultural AI / Protocol Atlas
- RN-03 Voice AI / Sahara / AssemblyAI / OMI

## Core invariant
NO LOAD-BEARING RESEARCH CLAIM WITHOUT TRACEABLE PROVENANCE.

Every meaningful update records what changed, why it changed, and the evidence causing the change.
