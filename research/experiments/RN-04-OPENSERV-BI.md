# RN-04 — OpenServ / Pan-African Intelligence Precision Study

Objective: measure whether LOG_ON can turn fresh public signals into verified, contactable and decision-useful opportunities without over-inference.

## Signal lifecycle
DISCOVER
→ SOURCE VERIFY
→ CLAIM EXTRACT
→ MARKET ENRICH
→ OPPORTUNITY TRANSLATE
→ CONTACT ROUTE
→ HUMAN REVIEW
→ ACTION
→ OUTCOME

## Evidence separation
Observation = explicitly reported by a source.
Inference = reasoned interpretation of evidence.
Opportunity = proposed LOG_ON value from a verified bottleneck.
Action = human-approved external step.

Never collapse these into one generated field.

## Seven-day pilot
Markets:
Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, Rwanda, Uganda, Tanzania.

Feeds:
1. official regulator updates
2. company/investor announcements
3. EAC/PAPSS/regional infrastructure
4. reputable business/technology reporting

Target:
5–10 verified signals/day and enrich top 3/day.

## Metrics
- discovery precision
- verification precision
- unsupported-claim rate
- expansion-intent accuracy
- contactability rate
- opportunity acceptance rate
- action approval rate
- downstream response rate

## Current evidence model
The existing Pan-African module already defines signal taxonomy, evidence hierarchy, country objects, relationship graph, scoring dimensions and a six-market thin slice. This research run measures whether those design rules produce accurate live intelligence.

## Safety
Never invent contacts, regulatory requirements, payment rails, licensing details or expansion intent. Social/newsletter material is discovery unless independently verified.
